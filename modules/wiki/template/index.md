# Wiki index

The secretary reads this page and `rules.md` on every request. Keep both short.

- [profile.md](profile.md): who you are, goals and priorities
- [rules.md](rules.md): how your time should be organized
- [decisions.md](decisions.md): dated log of decisions and their reasons
- courses/: one page per course (optional)
- projects/: one page per project (optional)
- journal/: one page per weekly review, named YYYY-Www.md
